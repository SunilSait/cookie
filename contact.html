Frequently Asked

Do you offer custom cookie designs?

Yes! We create custom cookie designs for weddings, birthdays, corporate events, and any special occasion. Contact us at least 5 days in advance.

What are your delivery areas?

We offer same-day delivery within 25 miles and ship nationwide with 2-day express. All orders are packaged in insulated boxes to ensure freshness.

Do you cater to dietary restrictions?

Absolutely! We offer gluten-free, vegan, nut-free, and sugar-free options. Just let us know your requirements and we'll make something special for you.

What's the minimum order for wholesale?

Our wholesale minimum starts at 100 cookies per order with discounted pricing. We work with cafes, restaurants, and retailers. Email us for a wholesale price list.
