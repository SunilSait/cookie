The Beginning

From a Home Kitchen to Your Heart

CookieCraft began in 2020 when founder Maya Chen started baking cookies for her neighbors during the lockdown. What started as a simple act of kindness quickly grew into a beloved local brand. Maya's secret? Using only the finest organic ingredients and baking with genuine love.

Today, we've grown from a small home kitchen to a state-of-the-art artisan bakery, but our philosophy remains the same: every cookie should taste like it was made just for you, because it was.

Our Process

From raw ingredients to your doorstep.

01

Source Premium Ingredients

We hand-select organic flour, European butter, free-range eggs, and the finest Belgian chocolate from trusted farms and suppliers.

02

Handcraft Each Batch

Our bakers mix and shape every cookie by hand, ensuring the perfect texture and flavor balance that machines simply can't replicate.

03

Bake to Perfection

Each batch is baked at precisely the right temperature and timing, creating that signature crispy outside and chewy inside texture.

04

Deliver Fresh to You

Packaged in our eco-friendly boxes and delivered same-day, so your cookies arrive as fresh as the moment they left the oven.
